2. Confidential Information

2.1"Confidential Information" means all non-public information of the Company, its parent Nablon AI Private Limited, its affiliates and its clients that you receive or access during the internship, in any form, including source code, models, algorithms, data sets, product plans, client information, business and financial information, and any notes or materials derived from them.

2.3These obligations do not apply to information that (a) is or becomes public through no fault of yours; (b) you lawfully knew before receiving it from the Company, as shown by written records; or (c) you lawfully receive from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

3. Intellectual Property

3.1All work product, inventions, code, documentation, data, models and other materials that you create, alone or with others, in the course of the internship or using Company resources ("Work Product") are the sole property of the Company and, to the extent permitted by law, are "works made for hire" under the U.S. Copyright Act.

3.2To the extent any Work Product is not a work made for hire, you hereby assign to the Company all of your right, title and interest in it, including all intellectual property rights, and you agree to sign any documents reasonably needed to confirm or register the Company's ownership.

3.3You will not incorporate into any Work Product any third-party material or any of your own prior work without the Company's written approval.

4. No Reverse Engineering and Information Security

4.1You will not reverse engineer, decompile or disassemble any software, model or system of the Company or its clients, or attempt to derive source code or underlying data from it.

4.2You will follow the Company's information security policies, use only Company-approved accounts, devices and tools for Company work, not copy Confidential Information to personal devices or accounts, and not input Confidential Information into any AI tool or external service that the Company has not approved in writing.

5.1If you are required by law, subpoena or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, you will, where legally permitted, give the Company prompt written notice so it can seek a protective order, and you will disclose only the portion legally required.

5.2You acknowledge that a breach of this letter may cause the Company irreparable harm, and that the Company may seek injunctive relief in addition to any other remedy available at law or in equity, without the need to post a bond to the extent permitted by law.

5.3Nothing in this letter prevents you from reporting possible violations of law to a government agency or from making disclosures protected under whistleblower laws. Under the Defend Trade Secrets Act, 18 U.S.C. 1833(b), you will not be held criminally or civilly liable for disclosing a trade secret in confidence to a government official or an attorney solely for the purpose of reporting or investigating a suspected violation of law, or in a complaint or other document filed under seal in a lawsuit.

6.1At the end of the internship, or earlier on the Company's request, you will promptly return or, if the Company so directs, permanently delete all Confidential Information and Company property in your possession, and will confirm in writing that you have done so.
